### Metric Definitions
1. **Avg Gemini Call (s):** Average time taken by Gemini to run Google Maps grounding and output the structured JSON response containing the places, PlaceIDs, and CIDs.
2. **Avg Places API Call (s):** Average time taken to verify all PlaceIDs and CIDs in parallel against their respective Google APIs.
3. **Grounded Rate (%):** The percentage of successful Gemini requests that contained any grounding metadata chunks returned from Google Maps.
4. **Gen PlaceID Verif (%):** The percentage of model-generated places where the directly-generated `PlaceId` was valid AND the fuzzy match score was \ge 85%.
5. **Grounded PlaceID Verif (%):** The percentage of model-generated places where the `grounded_place_id` (extracted from the grounding metadata) was valid AND the fuzzy match score was \ge 85%.
6. **CID Verif (%):** The percentage of model-generated places where the numeric `CID` was valid AND the fuzzy match score was \ge 85%.
